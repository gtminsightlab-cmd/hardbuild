# Landing Page Optimization audit — checks

**Purpose:** Optimize the landing page to drive the main call to action: a visitor understands who it is for and what they get within seconds, sees one clear next step, and that step actually works.
**Scorecard row:** Landing Page Optimization · **Report:** `audits/landing-page.md` · **Overlaps:** Copy & Content (claim-vs-code for every promise, wording), SEO (titles, OG, indexability), Performance (full CWV), Analytics (event taxonomy), Accessibility (contrast, focus), Legal (disclaimers on insurance claims). Reference, don't repeat.

## Inputs to gather first
- `.hardbuild.json → audit.primaryCta` (e.g. `"Get a quote"`, `"Start free trial"`, `"Book a demo"`) and `audit.productionUrl`. If `primaryCta` is empty, infer it from the hero button and state the assumption at the top of the report.
- The landing route: `app/page.tsx` (or `app/(marketing)/page.tsx`) and every component it imports; any dedicated LPs under `app/(marketing)/lp/*` or `app/for/*`.
- The CTA target: the form component, server action/route handler, Supabase table or external endpoint (HubSpot, Growtheon, Resend, Cal.com) it posts to, and the success/thank-you route.
- Analytics setup: `grep -rln "posthog\|gtag\|@vercel/analytics\|plausible\|segment\|mixpanel" app components lib`.

## Checks
Format: `ID · weight · check · how to verify (strongest first) · typical failure`

- **LPO-01 · 3 · Hero says who it is for and the outcome in ≤ 12 words.**
  VERIFY: READ the hero `<h1>` and the first supporting line in `app/page.tsx` (or its `Hero` component); count words; it must name the audience (independent agents, PEO brokers, wholesale producers) and the result (bind faster, quote WC in minutes), not the category ("AI-powered platform"). With a URL: `curl -s <productionUrl> | grep -oE "<h1[^>]*>.*?</h1>" | sed 's/<[^>]*>//g'`.
  FAIL PATTERN: `"The modern platform for insurance innovation"` (no audience, no outcome, 6 words of nothing); hero copy that only an insider could decode.
- **LPO-02 · 3 · One primary CTA above the fold, repeated at the end, nothing competing.**
  VERIFY: READ the hero: exactly one visually primary button whose label equals `primaryCta` (secondary "Watch demo"/"See pricing" allowed as text or ghost button). `grep -n "primaryCta text\|<Button" app/page.tsx components/marketing/*.tsx` → count primary-variant buttons above the fold (expect 1) and confirm the same CTA appears in the final section and the sticky header. With a browser: screenshot at 1440×900 and 390×844; the primary CTA must be visible without scrolling in both.
  FAIL PATTERN: three equally weighted buttons ("Sign up", "Request demo", "Contact sales"); CTA label differs between hero and footer; CTA pushed below the fold by an announcement bar plus nav plus oversized hero image.
- **LPO-03 · 3 · The primary CTA works end-to-end (trace the code path).**
  VERIFY: start at the CTA element → `href`/`onClick` → destination route or form component → submit handler (`"use server"` action, `app/api/*/route.ts`, or third-party embed) → persistence (Supabase `insert` into `leads`/`quote_requests`, CRM API call, email via Resend) → success state (redirect to `/thank-you`, toast, or confirmation email). Confirm each hop exists and is wired (`grep -rn "action={\|onSubmit\|fetch('/api" components/marketing`). With a URL and a browser: submit the form with a clearly marked test payload (`audit-test+<date>@example.com`) only if the owner approved test submissions in `.hardbuild.json → audit.allowTestSubmissions`; otherwise stop at the code trace and mark READ. Check the Supabase table/CRM for the row, or Vercel runtime logs (`mcp__Vercel__get_runtime_logs`) for the handler invocation.
  FAIL PATTERN: `<Button>Get a quote</Button>` with no `href` or handler; form posts to `/api/lead` which returns 200 and does nothing (`// TODO: wire CRM`); signup goes to Supabase Auth but email confirmation is on and SMTP is not configured, so nobody ever gets the link.
- **LPO-04 · 2 · Social proof is real (claim-vs-code).**
  VERIFY: `grep -rniE "logo-?(1|2|3|placeholder)|acme|lorem|john doe|jane doe|customer name|testimonial placeholder|\"\\*\\*\\*\\*\"|5,?000\\+ (agencies|customers|users)" app components public` → every hit is a finding. For each logo/quote/metric: name, company, and role must be attributable (a `testimonials` table, a CMS entry, or a signed-off static list with a source comment). Numbers like "2,000+ agents" need a source (DB count, CRM export) or a hedge.
  FAIL PATTERN: six greyscale SVG logos of companies that are not customers; a testimonial from "Sarah M., Agency Owner" with no company; "Trusted by 500+ agencies" at week two.
- **LPO-05 · 2 · Pricing clarity or an honest "talk to sales".**
  VERIFY: `/pricing` route exists (`ls app/pricing app/(marketing)/pricing 2>/dev/null`) with tiers, billing period, and what is included; or the landing page says plainly that pricing is quote-based and the CTA leads to a scheduling flow. If prices are shown, cross-check against Stripe/billing config (`grep -rn "price_" lib app | head`) — Billing owns correctness, LPO owns that the page does not contradict it.
  FAIL PATTERN: "Simple, transparent pricing" header followed by three "Contact us" cards; a "$49/mo" badge while Stripe has only a $99 price.
- **LPO-06 · 2 · Objections are handled: FAQ, security, compliance, integrations.**
  VERIFY: READ landing sections for an FAQ (≥ 5 real questions prospects ask: data ownership, carrier appointments needed, E&O/compliance, state availability, onboarding time), a security/trust block (SOC 2 status honestly stated, encryption, Supabase/Vercel hosting), and integration logos that are actually integrated (cross-check `lib/integrations/*`). Insurance-specific: statements about coverage, rates, or binding must carry the disclaimer Legal requires.
  FAIL PATTERN: no FAQ; "Bank-level security, SOC 2 compliant" with no report or in-progress note; "Integrates with your AMS" with zero AMS code.
- **LPO-07 · 2 · Landing page loads fast (LCP ≤ 2.5 s mobile).**
  VERIFY: `npx lighthouse <productionUrl> --only-categories=performance --form-factor=mobile --screenEmulation.mobile --output=json --quiet | jq '.audits."largest-contentful-paint".numericValue, .audits."cumulative-layout-shift".numericValue'`; Vercel Speed Insights if enabled. READ fallback: hero image uses `next/image` with `priority` and explicit `sizes`; fonts via `next/font`; no client-side-only hero (`"use client"` on the hero component is a smell); third-party scripts via `next/script` `strategy="afterInteractive"`/`lazyOnload`. Deep analysis belongs to Performance; cite it.
  FAIL PATTERN: 2.4 MB autoplaying hero video; chat widget and three tag managers loaded `beforeInteractive`.
- **LPO-08 · 2 · Mobile hero fits one screen with the CTA visible.**
  VERIFY: browser screenshot at 390×844 (iPhone 14/15 class) → headline, one line of support, and the primary CTA visible without scrolling; no horizontal scroll (`document.documentElement.scrollWidth <= window.innerWidth`); tap targets ≥ 44 px. READ fallback: hero container uses `min-h-[100svh]`/`min-h-dvh` or fixed padding, text sizes scale (`text-4xl md:text-6xl`), nav collapses to a menu below `md`.
  FAIL PATTERN: desktop `text-7xl` headline wraps to seven lines on mobile pushing the CTA two screens down; cookie banner covers the CTA.
- **LPO-09 · 2 · Form friction is minimal and protected.**
  VERIFY: READ the CTA form: count required fields (≤ 4 for a lead form: name, work email, company, one qualifier like "states you write in"; a signup should be email + password or magic link only); inline validation with `zod` + `react-hook-form` or native constraints, errors shown next to fields; spam protection present (Cloudflare Turnstile/hCaptcha, honeypot field, or Upstash rate limit on the handler — `grep -rn "turnstile\|hcaptcha\|honeypot\|ratelimit" components app`); work-email check if B2B-only.
  FAIL PATTERN: 11 required fields including phone and "how did you hear about us" before any value; no spam control so the `leads` table fills with bot rows; errors only in a toast after submit.
- **LPO-10 · 2 · Conversion events fire on the CTA and on success.**
  VERIFY: `grep -rnE "track\(|gtag\(|posthog\.capture\(|plausible\(|analytics\.track\(|sendGAEvent\(" components app lib` → an event on primary CTA click (`cta_clicked` with `location: hero|footer|nav`), on form submit, and on the success page (`lead_submitted`/`signup_completed`), with the same names used in the analytics dashboard. With a browser: click the CTA and read `read_network_requests` filtered by the analytics host (`/e/`, `/collect`, `/i/v0/e`) → request fires. Analytics area owns the taxonomy; here confirm the CTA is measured at all.
  FAIL PATTERN: page views tracked, zero custom events, so conversion rate is unknowable; events fire before the server confirms success.
- **LPO-11 · 1 · Message matches ads, SEO title, and social preview.**
  VERIFY: compare the `<h1>`, `<title>`, `og:title`, and `og:description` of the landing route (`curl -s <productionUrl> | grep -oE "<title>[^<]*|og:(title|description)\" content=\"[^\"]*"`) with each other and with any ad copy or LinkedIn/Google Ads headlines the owner supplies; the promise and the audience must be the same across all of them. Dedicated LPs (`/lp/*`, `?utm_campaign=`) should restate the campaign's headline.
  FAIL PATTERN: ad says "Instant WC quotes for PEO brokers", landing says "Modern HR solutions for growing businesses".
- **LPO-12 · 2 · No dead links or broken assets.**
  VERIFY: `npx linkinator <productionUrl> --recurse --skip "mailto:|tel:" --format csv | grep -vE ",200,|,OK" | head -40` → list every non-200 internal link and any broken image (`curl -sI` on `og:image`, hero image, logos). READ fallback: `grep -rnoE 'href="(/[^"]*|#[^"]*)"' app/page.tsx components/marketing | sort -u` → confirm each path has a matching `app/**/page.tsx` and each `#anchor` has a matching `id=`.
  FAIL PATTERN: footer links to `/privacy` and `/terms` that 404; `href="#"` placeholders on nav items; "Read the docs" → `docs.example.com` that never got a DNS record.
- **LPO-13 · 2 · Thank-you / next-step page exists and sets expectations.**
  VERIFY: after the CTA handler, `redirect('/thank-you')` or an inline success state exists (`ls app/thank-you app/(marketing)/thank-you app/welcome 2>/dev/null`; `grep -rn "redirect(\|router.push(" components/marketing/*Form*`); the page states what happens next and when ("We'll email your indication within one business day"), offers a secondary action (book a call, read the guide), and carries the conversion event from LPO-10. Confirmation email template exists (`grep -rln "resend.emails.send\|sendEmail" lib app`) and is sent from a real domain.
  FAIL PATTERN: form clears with no feedback; success toast disappears in 3 s and the user re-submits; thank-you page indexable and ranking (should be `noindex`).
- **LPO-14 · 1 · Dedicated LPs minimize exit paths.**
  VERIFY: N/A if there are no campaign-specific landing pages (`app/(marketing)/lp/*`, `/for/*`, `/demo`). Otherwise READ their layout: no full site nav or mega-menu, footer trimmed to legal links, one CTA repeated, no links to blog/careers; UTM parameters preserved through to the form payload (`grep -rn "utm_" components lib` → captured and stored on the lead row).
  FAIL PATTERN: paid-traffic LP reuses the marketing layout with 14 nav links and a blog carousel; UTMs dropped on the redirect to `/signup`.

## Scoring notes
- LPO-03 failing (CTA does not complete, or completes but stores/sends nothing) → CRITICAL: the core job of the landing page does not work (grade cap D). LPO-04 with fabricated logos/testimonials → CRITICAL for legal exposure (also report under Legal/Copy).
- LPO-02 and LPO-08 failing together → HIGH ("broken on mobile" per scoring.md).
- Without `productionUrl`, LPO-07/08/10/12 are READ at best (conf 85) from components and config; LPO-03 is READ from the code trace — state explicitly that no live submission was made. Never run a live submission unless `audit.allowTestSubmissions` is true; a real lead row created by the audit is itself a finding to clean up.
- "To raise confidence": production URL without Deployment Protection; `allowTestSubmissions` plus Supabase MCP (or CRM access) to confirm the lead row; browser access for mobile screenshots and network-request capture; the owner's current ad headlines for LPO-11; Lighthouse from the Performance run for LPO-07.
